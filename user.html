Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a service appointment?
You can book a service appointment through the "Book New Service" section in your dashboard. Select your vehicle, preferred date and time, and choose the service you need.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, cash, and financing options for larger repairs.
How can I track my vehicle's repair status?
Go to "Track Repair Status" in your dashboard to see real-time updates on your vehicle's repair progress from confirmation to pickup readiness.
What is your cancellation policy?
You can cancel or reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the scheduled time at no charge. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a $50 fee.
Do you offer warranty on repairs?
Yes! We offer a 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty on all parts and labor. If you experience any issues, we'll fix them at no additional cost.
