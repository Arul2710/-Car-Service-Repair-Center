Our Story

Building Trust, One Vehicle at a Time

Founded in 2011, Car Service & Repair Center started as a modest three-bay workshop with a simple mission: provide honest, reliable automotive care to our community. What began as a family-owned garage has grown into a full-service automotive facility spanning over 15,000 square feet.

Today, we operate with more than 20 service bays staffed by 35+ certified technicians. Our team has serviced over 50,000 vehicles, building a reputation for transparency, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction that keeps families and businesses coming back generation after generation.

We invest continuously in the latest diagnostic equipment and training programs to ensure every vehicle receives manufacturer-level attention. From routine oil changes to complex engine rebuilds, our commitment remains the same — treat every car as if it were our own.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional automotive repair and maintenance services with complete transparency, using the latest technology and genuine parts. We aim to extend the life of every vehicle that enters our facility while building lasting relationships rooted in trust and honesty.

Our Vision

To become the region's most trusted name in automotive care by setting new standards for service quality, environmental responsibility, and customer experience. We envision a future where every driver has access to reliable, affordable, and professional vehicle maintenance.
